PM Insight Report — Atlys Overall Product and Funnel Performance

Scope: Overall PM view across the core visa funnel and available feature tables in the atlys database. The primary reliable window is January–June 2026; July contains only a small data tail.

1. Executive readout

  • Demand is growing: applications increased from 21,580 in January to 30,370 in June, a 40.7% increase.
  • Overall conversion remains constrained by two downstream steps: application_started → document_uploaded is 13.2%, document_uploaded → pay_now_clicked is 72.1%, and pay_now_clicked → purchase_completed is 47.9%.
  • The highest-value product problem is payment completion: 7,685 payment-intent applications did not reach purchase completion in the observed data.
  • Android needs targeted attention: its document-to-payment rate is 67.1% versus 75.5% on iOS, while failed-capture threshold crossing is 16.2% versus 8.7% on iOS.
  • Group applications degrade sharply with group size: submission falls from 69.5% for two-person groups to 31.1% for six-person groups.
  • Do not prioritize unsupported narratives: the iOS OTP regression, SUMMER20 Q2 spike, and app 7.45 rollout are contradicted by the current data. WhatsApp recovery performance cannot be trusted until the recovery table is repaired.

The start-to-document step is the largest absolute funnel loss, with roughly 133,967 started applications not represented by a document upload. However, this top-of-funnel loss may include browsing, incomplete intent, and acquisition mix. The more actionable money-funnel leak is pay-now to purchase, where nearly half of payment-intent applications fail to complete.

3. Growth and commercial performance

Monthly application volume rose from 21,580 in January to 30,370 in June. Monthly pay-to-purchase conversion stayed relatively stable between 46.6% and 49.8%, indicating that growth has not yet produced a clear deterioration in payment conversion. June generated 1,203 purchases from 2,526 pay-now applications, compared with 1,062 purchases from 2,132 pay-now applications in January.

Purchase values are recorded in transaction currency and should not be summed across currencies without FX normalization. INR dominates recorded purchase count with 3,791 purchases and approximately INR 19.1 million in gross value. Other high-volume currencies include AED, USD, and GBP.

Android is the clearest platform-specific opportunity: it is approximately 8.4 percentage points below iOS on document-to-payment progression and has an almost 1.9x iOS rate of crossing the failed-attempt threshold. PM action should be to audit Android capture UX, camera permissions, image quality, OCR feedback, and retry recovery before assuming a payment-only issue.

The known iOS OTP-autofill hypothesis is not supported. iOS has the highest observed pay-to-purchase conversion among the major OS groups, and prior cohort analysis found Gulf iOS performance above non-Gulf iOS across app versions.

5. Express checkout

Express checkout was shown to 1,650 applications. Of these, 1,007 selected express checkout and used a saved method/entered OTP, while 836 reached payment confirmation. This implies approximately 83.0% confirmation among the selected cohort. Observed payment latency for confirmed express payments averaged about 2.3 seconds.

This is a promising conversion lever, but the event stream is small relative to the core funnel. Recommended next step: instrument eligibility reasons, OTP failure reasons, cancellation, and downstream purchase linkage so PMs can measure incremental conversion rather than only event progression.

6. Coupons and pricing

The coupon funnel recorded 2,100 field views, 848 entries, 580 applications, and 268 rejections. The observed application success rate after entry was approximately 68.4%. Checkout-with-coupon events recorded approximately 190,435 in aggregate discount and 4,985,822 in final checkout value in the source currency mix; these totals should not be interpreted as a single-currency commercial figure.

The SUMMER20 issue is contradicted on timing: prior analysis found redemptions broadly flat at approximately 55–65 per month rather than showing a Q2 spike. Treat couponing as a persistent pricing/retention capability, not evidence of a discrete seasonal campaign lift.

Group-size friction is one of the strongest product signals in the feature data. Submission rate declines monotonically as traveller count increases. Prioritize bulk traveller entry, save-and-resume, clearer document-status visibility, and proactive completion reminders for larger groups. This is a stronger near-term product opportunity than adding more generic acquisition volume.

8. Visa-status sharing

The sharing table contains 1,144 link-generated shares, 1,600 share-clicked events, 2,310 link-opened events, and 305 recipient CTA clicks. Channel data shows WhatsApp as the largest populated channel by event volume, followed by copy link, email, and SMS. However, recipient attribution has unusual user-count behavior and should be validated at the share-level grain before using it for growth or referral claims.

Recommended PM action: define share-level metrics explicitly—generated share, unique open, recipient CTA, application started, and purchase—then deduplicate raw events by event_id before trusting aggregates.

9. Abandonment and recovery

The abandonment_recovery_events table contains 5,919 rows, mostly in June, but it has a confirmed column-shift data-integrity defect. Values do not reliably correspond to declared columns, and aggregate views built from the landed data are not authoritative. Therefore, WhatsApp-versus-other recovery effectiveness cannot currently be reported.

Priority is remediation and reconciliation: validated staging, event deduplication, field validation, high-watermark cutover, corrected backfill, post-cutover replay, and atomic replacement. Do not make channel-budget or lifecycle decisions from the current recovery table.

10. Known-issue assessment

  • K1 contradicted: no evidence of an iOS OTP regression; iOS pay-to-purchase performance is strongest.
  • K2 partially supported: Android has materially higher capture-failure threshold crossing, but the April timing claim is not yet proven.
  • K3 untested: citizenship and retry analysis is needed as a proxy for passport-script effects.
  • K4 untested: Schengen-only seasonal analysis is required.
  • K5 untested/blocked: recovery table exists, but its column-shift defect prevents trustworthy channel analysis.
  • K6 contradicted on timing: SUMMER20 redemptions are flat rather than Q2-spiking.
  • K7 contradicted: app versions show approximately equal monthly shares rather than a visible 7.45 rollout curve.

11. PM prioritization

  1. Fix Android document capture: investigate threshold crossing and document-to-payment loss by capture mode, device model, retry count, and citizenship.
  2. Improve payment completion: instrument failure reasons around OTP, payment method, bank decline, latency, and checkout abandonment; target the 47.9% pay-to-purchase step.
  3. Scale group completion: design for larger groups with batch workflows, persistent progress, and targeted reminders.
  4. Operationalize express checkout: connect express events to purchase outcomes and measure incremental conversion.
  5. Repair recovery data before experimentation: do not evaluate WhatsApp recovery or allocate channel spend using the corrupted landed table.
  6. Establish metric governance: normalize currency for revenue reporting, deduplicate sharing events before aggregation, and maintain application_id-matched funnel definitions.

12. Caveats

The original event envelope has approximately 3% duplicate_id presence, approximately 2% back-filled rows, and some NULL OS values concentrated among Android rows. Several newer feature tables have repeat-event grains and must not be treated as one-row-per-user. Timestamp ordering is not reliable for later funnel events, so the report uses application_id-matched distinct counts rather than sequence-based funnel functions.
