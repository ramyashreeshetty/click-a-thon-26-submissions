Promo / Coupon Checkout

Executive takeaway: Coupons are not yet demonstrating conversion lift in this observational cut. Users who entered a coupon reached coded checkout at 43.2%, below the 49.6% no-coupon baseline by 6.4 percentage points.

Segment findings

Apply rates were highest on web Windows (31.4%) and iOS (29.1%) among the larger segments, and lowest on Android (23.5%). Geography showed little spread in apply rates, while destination-level checkout progression varied more: US reached 81.4% of applied users to coded checkout, compared with 52.6% in FR.

Recommended actions

  1. Investigate why coupon enterers underperform the baseline before scaling discounts; run a randomized coupon exposure or holdout test.
  2. Prioritize validation messaging and eligibility UX for minimum-cart and already-used rejections.
  3. Audit EXPIRED5 distribution and suppress or replace it if it is intentionally expired.
  4. Review SUMMER20 margin economics and test lower-cost alternatives against conversion outcomes.
  5. Break out destination and device-specific offers, especially where apply-to-checkout progression is weak.
