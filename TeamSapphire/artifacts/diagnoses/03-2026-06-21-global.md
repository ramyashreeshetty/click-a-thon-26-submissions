# Incident 3 — 2026-06-21 00:00:00 → 2026-06-21 23:00:00

**Classification:** `global` · **24h** · severity 11.2 · primary factor **requests**

## Diagnosis

> Revenue -44.8%, driven by requests falling uniformly across every dimension — no single segment responsible

### In plain language

Revenue fell 44.81% over the 24-hour window from 2026-06-21 00:00:00 to 2026-06-21 23:00:00. The movement is almost entirely an upstream volume story: requests came in at 126,052 against a baseline of 223,079, a 43.49% decline accounting for 96.03% of the total movement. eCPM contributed a marginal 4.02% (2.4186 vs. 2.4771, -2.36%), while fill rate (0.7855 vs. 0.7849, +0.08%) and render rate (0.9794 vs. 0.9798, -0.05%) were effectively flat.

No segment is responsible — the drop was uniform across every dimension tested. The shape is the informative part: requests stepped from 6,503 to 3,651 in the single hour at 2026-06-21 00:00:00, with 100% of the movement landing in that one hour, and stepped back from 3,603 to 8,132 at 2026-06-22 00:00:00. Both transitions are clean steps landing exactly on a day boundary, and the episode lasted exactly 24 hours. Interpretation: that is the signature of something that schedules in whole days — a campaign flight with a defined end date, a config push, or a nightly job — rather than an organic degradation or a fix that was applied.

Ruled out: publisher_tier (3 values, all within 0.7% of the global -43.5%), ad_format (5 values, within 1.0%), category (7 values, within 1.4%), vertical (8 values, within 2.2%), os_version (8 values, within 0.8%), device_model (8 values, within 1.0%), region (5 values, within 0.7%), country (16 values, within 2.9%), and campaign_type (4 values, within 0.2%) — in every case the largest outlier explains at most 0.3% of the incident. Also ruled out is a supply or demand-quality problem: fill rate, render rate and eCPM held steady throughout, so everything that did arrive filled and rendered normally — fewer requests simply arrived. Next step is to look for day-scheduled changes effective 2026-06-21 and ending 2026-06-22: campaign flight dates, scheduled config or traffic-shaping pushes, and any nightly job that gates request volume.
