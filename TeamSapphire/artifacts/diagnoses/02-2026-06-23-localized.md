Both transitions land exactly on a day boundary and the episode lasted exactly 3 days. Interpretation: that is consistent with something scheduled in days — a campaign flight with a defined end date, a config push, a nightly job — rather than an organic degradation or a fix.

The evidence rules out a traffic or tracking problem: request volume was unchanged, so the same demand arrived and was handled differently. Next step is to look for whatever was scheduled to start 2026-06-23 00:00:00 and end 2026-06-26 00:00:00 with Android 15 targeting — bidder/demand eligibility rules, an SDK or OS-version filter, or a campaign flight — with EU as a secondary lens.
