## Diagnosis

> Revenue +0.5%, driven by requests in campaign_type=CPC (+139.5% vs +8.6% across the platform)

### In plain language

Between 2026-07-06 00:00:00 and 2026-07-08 22:00:00 — a 71-hour window — revenue was essentially flat at +0.52%, but the composition underneath it moved sharply. Requests rose +8.61% (902,169 vs a baseline of 830,662), accounting for 15.79 of the movement share, while eCPM fell -7.39% (2.2848 vs 2.467) for -14.67 share, largely offsetting it. Fill rate (0.7733 vs 0.7737, -0.04%) and render rate (0.9799 vs 0.9801, -0.02%) were unchanged.

The movement is localized, and the primary factor is requests. The sharpest segment is campaign_type=CPC, which moved +139.5% against +9.3% globally, carrying 97% of the unexplained movement and 217% of the whole incident. Correlated slices show the same population from other angles: category=news +201.9%, os_version=iOS 17.5 +187.9%, device_model=iPhone 14 +92.6%, region=APAC +43.5%, publisher_tier=tier_2 +28.5% (100% of unexplained movement), and vertical=ecommerce +59.2%. The shape within CPC was gradual, not a single step: hourly requests went from 1,056 to 2,953 (+1,897) with the largest single hour carrying 62% of the change, and the onset lands exactly on the 2026-07-06 00:00:00 day boundary. It recovers gradually from 2026-07-09 04:00:00 (3,161 to 3,515, +354, step fraction 0.2659), off any day boundary. Interpretation: a gradual ramp anchored to a day boundary is consistent with a rollout or something that schedules in days — a campaign flight, a config push, a nightly job — rather than an organic failure.

Checked and cleared: ad_format is uniform — all 5 values moved within 0.7% of the global +8.6%, and the largest outlier explains only 0.6% of the incident. Country is inconclusive: ID leads, but its excess is 45% of the unexplained movement, below the 50% concentration bar, so the movement is spread across values rather than concentrated. Because fill_rate and render_rate held steady through the window, a supply or demand-quality problem is ruled out — everything that arrived filled and rendered normally. Next, look at what started on CPC at 2026-07-06 00:00:00: campaign flight starts or budget/pacing changes on CPC in tier_2 APAC inventory, and why the added CPC volume monetized at a lower eCPM (2.2848 vs 2.467) such that revenue barely moved.
